Questions

Good to know

Does it work in every app?

Almost every one: browsers, email, chat apps, documents, spreadsheets and code editors. If an app blocks typing from outside, Keeyz shows the result in a small window so you can copy it.

Shortcut or typing “Keeyz” – which should I use?

The shortcut is best for text you've already written: select it and pick an action. Typing “Keeyz” is quickest while you're writing, because your hands never leave the keyboard.

Do I need my own AI key?

Yes. That's what keeps Keeyz free and unlimited. Google Gemini gives a free key in a minute, and Auto mode finds the quickest model your key can use.

Is my text used for training?

Keeyz never sees your text – it goes straight to the provider you picked, under that provider's terms. Most paid API plans don't train on your data.

Does it work offline?

Reading text off the screen and the clipboard history work offline. Writing help needs an internet connection, unless you run a local model on your PC.

Which languages are supported?

Translation and voice typing cover more than 50 languages, and every other action answers in the language you wrote in.

Which systems does it run on?

Windows 10 and 11 today. Other systems may follow.

My antivirus warned me about the installer.

New programs without a paid code-signing certificate sometimes get flagged. Download only from this site or the official GitHub releases. Windows may ask you to click “More info” and then “Run anyway”.

How do updates work?

Keeyz checks for new versions by itself and shows an “Update” button. One click installs it and restarts the app – your settings stay.
