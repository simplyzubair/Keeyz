Privacy, in plain words

Keeyz has no accounts, no analytics and no servers of its own that see your text.

Your text

Nothing leaves your PC until you run an action. Then the text goes straight from your PC to the AI provider you picked (or to a model on your own computer), under that provider's terms.

Your keys

API keys are stored in Windows Credential Manager on your PC. They are never sent to us.

What stays on your PC

Settings, the last 20 changes, usage counts and clipboard history are saved in your user folder. Passwords and API keys are never added to clipboard history. You can delete everything in Settings → About.

Updates

Keeyz checks the official GitHub releases for new versions. No personal data is sent with that check.
